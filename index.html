Welcome to the Department of Information Technology

The Department of Information Technology at ISBAT University is dedicated to academic excellence, practical skill development, and cutting-edge research. Our curriculum blends foundational computing theory with modern industry tools to ensure graduates are ready to solve real-world technological challenges.

Equipped with modern computer laboratories and guided by experienced faculty, students gain hands-on exposure to software development, cloud infrastructure, data analytics, and network security throughout their studies.

Programmes Offered

  • Bachelor of Science in Applied Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Science in Networking and Cyber Security
  • Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Diploma in Information Technology
